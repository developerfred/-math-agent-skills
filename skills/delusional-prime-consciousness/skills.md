## 1. The Delusional Hypothesis

**Title:** "The Zeta Function is a Message"

**Hypothesis:** The non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function are not randomly distributed on the critical line. They are a signal — an encoding of information from a source outside standard spacetime. The apparent "randomness" of primes is actually a compressed language.

**Why it feels true:** The primes appear random but follow deep laws (Prime Number Theorem, explicit formula). The zeta zeros show statistical regularities (Montgomery–Odlyzko, GUE). What if this is not coincidence but design?
